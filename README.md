One satirical sentence for the make.fun bounty
[u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7):
a lawmaker who can draft a 400-page bill to regulate artificial intelligence
overnight, yet still emails his staff a scanned photo of a Word document and
asks them to turn it into a PDF before lunch.

## Run

Requires Node 22 or later. No dependencies to install.

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when `OUTPUT_DIR` is set). The sentence is
219 characters, inside the bounty gate of 1 to 320 characters, and the
program refuses to write it if that ever stops being true.

## Entry

> Congress can draft a 400-page bill to regulate artificial intelligence overnight, yet the lawmaker who wrote it still emails his staff a scanned photo of a Word document and asks them to turn it into a PDF before lunch.
